# Chemistry 2202 Review

A single-file practice app for Chemistry 2202 (Newfoundland and Labrador curriculum): generated practice questions with worked solutions, printable practice sheets and answer keys, practice exams built to the course tables of specifications, and exports for Kahoot, Blooket and Brightspace.

Coverage: Unit 1 Stoichiometry, Unit 2 From Structures to Properties, Unit 3 Organic Chemistry (the 31 content indicators), plus a Science 1206 prior-learning review unit (isotopes, atomic structure, naming). The Integrated Skills indicators are not included: they are assessed in labs and projects.

Exam presets: Mid-course review covers Units 1 and 2 (50 marks: 30 selected response, 20 constructed response, weighted 45 marks to Unit 1 and 5 to Unit 2, cognitive levels 20/60/20, two-hour guide timer). The final review covers Unit 3, the material not on the mid-course.

Part of the CDLI review apps collection. Status: draft, pending QA.
